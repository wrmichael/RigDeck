Contact

Frequency and mode follow the radio until you type a frequency or press SSB / CW; your choice is kept until the contact is logged. Frequency in MHz (14.250), kHz (14250) or Hz. Empty reports log as 59 (voice) or 599 (CW). Macros need the radio in CW mode; edit them in Settings. {CALL} is the callsign box, {RST} the Sent box (599 is sent as 5NN), {MYCALL}, {GRID} and {REF} (park reference) come from Settings.

Every contact is written to two files: the current log and the archive. Clearing the current log leaves the archive untouched, so download the current log into your logging program, clear it, and the next download holds only new contacts. The table shows contacts made since power-up or the last clear.

Clock

Without internet or GPS the clock follows this browser's clock each time the page is open. If most stations show the same DT offset, nudge the clock until DT is near zero. With GPS on, change "GPS sentence delay" instead: lower it if DT values are mostly negative, raise it if they are mostly positive.
