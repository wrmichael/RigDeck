Contact

Frequency and mode follow the radio until you type a frequency or press SSB / CW; your choice is kept until the contact is logged. Frequency in MHz (14.250), kHz (14250) or Hz. Empty reports log as 59 (voice) or 599 (CW). Macros need the radio in CW mode; edit them in Settings. {CALL} is the callsign box, {RST} the Sent box (599 is sent as 5NN), {MYCALL}, {GRID} and {REF} (park reference) come from Settings.

Logbook

RigDeck remembers every contact it has sent to the logging program (Settings, "Send log to"). Back home from the field, press Send unsent and the contacts it has not sent yet go to the logger. With UDP, "sent" means it left the board; RigDeck cannot tell whether the logger was listening. TCP knows.

Times are UTC (HHMM or HHMMSS), dates YYYYMMDD, frequency in MHz (the band follows it). Clear a box to remove that field. Changes apply to both the current log and the archive.

Recently deleted

DateUTCCallBandModeDeleted

Deleted contacts are kept here for 7 days. Restore puts a contact back where it was (current log, archive or both).

Every contact is written to two files: the current log and the archive. Clearing the current log leaves the archive untouched, so download the current log into your logging program, clear it, and the next download holds only new contacts. Edit or Delete fixes a contact in both files. Each station callsign has its own log: change the callsign in Settings and RigDeck switches to that station's files. Free space and "Erase all logs" are under Settings, Device.

Wi-Fi chip

Firmware: …

Updates the firmware of the board's Wi-Fi chip with rigdeck-wifi-chip.bin (from the RigDeck release, or build it with build_wifi_chip.bat). Join the RigDeck hotspot and use 192.168.4.1 for this, keep the board powered, and allow about a minute. The chip keeps its old firmware unless the new one arrives complete, and RigDeck restarts when it is done.

Brightness changes at once. Contrast (100 % = normal) applies after a restart.

Logbook (contacts) and storage

With a microSD card (FAT32) in at start-up the logs live on the card, in the RigDeck folder, one set of files per station callsign (e.g. AC9HP_log.adi, AC9HP_archive.adi). Contacts logged in the board's memory move onto the card at that start. On the card, deleted contacts are kept for good in AC9HP_deleted.adi and a cleared current log is kept as AC9HP_deleted_date_time.adi. Insert or remove the card with the power off.

Console logs (for troubleshooting)

Clock

Without internet or GPS the clock follows this browser's clock each time the page is open. If most stations show the same DT offset, nudge the clock until DT is near zero. With GPS on, change "GPS sentence delay" instead: lower it if DT values are mostly negative, raise it if they are mostly positive.
